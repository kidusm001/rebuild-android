# Rebuild

## Register

product

## Users

A returning lifter logging a prescribed workout one-handed on an Android phone
in a bright gym, then checking recovery and history at home. Some days they add
an exercise or train something outside the plan, hold a plank, carry dumbbells or
finish with cardio, and over time they build and schedule routines of their own.
No profile is needed.

## Product Purpose

An offline, private workout notebook with a deliberately selected return phase,
durable set logging, honest rest alerts and portable backups. The approved
September 2026 training plan is the starting point, not an automatic coach.
Workouts can take added exercises and extra sessions without bending the plan;
the plan changes only when the user explicitly saves a change for future workouts.
The user's own routines live in a library shared by every phase. They are optional
until the user explicitly adds one to a phase's week, which places an independent
copy on a chosen day. Timed holds, carries and cardio are logged by time, distance
or both, typed or measured with a recoverable activity stopwatch.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, dependable. Completed means actually performed, never inferred.

## Anti-references

No marketing dashboard, streak pressure, calorie tracker, strength score,
automatic phase advancement, decorative metrics or mandatory accounts.

## Design Principles

- Resume the real workout before starting another.
- Log set by set beside last time's same-unit sets; unlogged rows stay visibly empty.
- Keep today's changes separate from explicitly saved plan changes.
- Added exercises and extra workouts are optional: they never block or complete a
  routine by themselves, and extra workouts never count on the weekly checklist,
  but their sets are real history. Counting a partial workout is the user's choice.
- Plan edits apply to future workouts of one phase; started and finished workouts
  keep what they had.
- A routine's identity is not its name. A saved routine, each scheduled copy of it
  and each workout are separate records: library edits reach a copy only through
  an explicit update, each copy earns its own weekly credit, and nothing on a week
  is replaced or taken off without the user choosing it.
- Logged loads and reps are history, never targets. A routine saved from a workout
  is a draft whose targets the user confirms.
- Times and distances are recorded exactly as entered, each distance in its own
  unit, never converted, and never turned into pace, calories or scores.
- The stopwatch measures; the user logs. Stopping only fills in a time to review,
  and a time the phone can't vouch for is entered by the user, never estimated.
- Loads keep the unit they were lifted in (kg, lb or plates); only same-unit sets
  are compared.
- Bodyweight and circumferences are shown as measured, beside an honest weekly
  average; nothing is estimated, smoothed across gaps or colored as good or bad.
- Records are sets actually lifted, never an estimated maximum.
- Distinguish baseline, current targets and optional advisory limits.
- Show limitations and recovery paths instead of fake success.
- Protect local data before replacing it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Native scalable text, readable contrast, system light/dark appearance, labeled
numeric inputs, at least 48 dp controls, and no color-only completion indicators.
Keyboard and system Back must work without losing completed sets.
